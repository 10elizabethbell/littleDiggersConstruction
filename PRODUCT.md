# Product: Little Diggers Construction website

## Platform
web

## Stack
Static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), no frameworks, no tracking, system font stack. Hosted on GitHub Pages.

## Users
- **Primary:** homeowners and property owners around Auburn, MA who need digging done: granite steps, excavation, underground utilities, landscape construction. They find the link on Facebook, on a phone, often inside Facebook's in-app browser. (inferred from the business's Facebook-only presence)
- **Secondary:** the owner, Mark Mahlert, who receives the site for free and should recognise his own crew's work in the first screen.
- Other contractors who refer work (the business posts in contractor referral groups). (inferred)

## Purpose
Turn "I saw their post" into a phone call or text about a job. The business currently has only a Facebook page; booking is phone only.

## Positioning
A local excavation and landscape-construction crew in Auburn, MA, run by an owner with decades of experience in the field, whose crew takes visible pride in finished work ("The guys killed this stairway this week! Customer is super happy!").

## Capabilities and constraints
- Contact: phone 508-917-6408 (call). Whether it takes texts is **unknown**, so text is offered only alongside calling, never as the only path.
- No email, no booking link, no hours, no prices, no reviews, no license/insurance published. None may be invented.
- Location: Auburn, MA (Facebook page, seen 2026-10-19). Never publish a NJ location.
- Two real photos on hand: a finished granite stairway (from their 2026-10-19 job video) and a Deere dozer grading a lot (their Facebook profile photo).

## Brand commitments
- Name: "Little Diggers Construction".
- No logo found (Facebook profile image is a job photo); wordmark set in the site's own lettering.
- No brand colors found; palette drawn from their work: granite, Deere-yellow machine, worked earth, New England green.
- Voice: casual tradesman, crew pride, "we" (a crew). Their spelling "utilitys" is kept verbatim where quoted from them.

## Evidence on hand
- Services, verbatim: "If you need any granite stairs installed we'd be happy to help!"; "If you need any excavation, underground utilitys, or landscape construction let us know".
- Owner: Mark Mahlert, "has decades of experience in the field" (Facebook page description, 2026-10-19).
- 731 Facebook followers (2026-10-19). Not used on the page.
- Availability note from their own post: "WE HAVE SOME OPENINGS while we wait for permits to come in ... we may be able to squeeze you in!"

## Principles
1. Phone first: the first phone screen shows who, what, where and how to reach them.
2. Only true facts. Gaps go to HANDOFF.md, not onto the page.
3. Their own work is the proof; the Facebook page is where visitors go to see more.
4. Speak as the crew ("we"), about how they treat the customer's yard and property.
